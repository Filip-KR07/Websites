IPhilosophy

Enjoy mindfully. Made with heart and high standards. Fresh, honest and made by hand.

Paledo is a café & deli on Mühlenkamp, right in the heart of Winterhude. Come for bowls, good coffee and homemade food – healthy, local and sustainable.

We only use ingredients we love ourselves: high quality, sustainable and full of flavour. Vegan, lactose-free, gluten-free, no additives or flavourings – we make a lot possible.

IVA day at Paledo

From the first espresso to the last bowl

On MühlenkampA place for good coffee, fresh bowls and a break from the day.

  1. 09:00

    Breakfast

    The doors open, the espresso machine warms up. Avocado toast, overnight oats or salmon Benedict – with a flat white by Public Coffee Roasters.

  2. 12:00

    Lunch

    Monday to Friday from twelve it’s the lunch menu: build your own bowl and add a Fritz Limo or water as a lunch deal.

  3. 15:00

    Matcha & sweets

    Iced strawberry matcha, fresh pancakes or a slice from the cake counter. The kitchen is open until 5 pm, and at weekends the café stays open until 6 pm.
